SHOW THE EVIDENCE. NAME THE LIMITS.

What this tool knows—and what it does not.

Guidance is informed by public consumer-protection sources. The rule set is an educational prototype, not a fraud-detection model.

Important limits

  • Rules can miss scams and flag legitimate text. Quoting advice, irony and unfamiliar wording can change the meaning.
  • Only configured English and Vietnamese text patterns are covered. No image, audio, identity or financial-transaction analysis is performed.
  • Links are parsed as text. There is no site visit, blacklist, domain ownership check or “safe” classification.
  • No victim-loss reduction, representative accuracy or user adoption has been measured.

Already sent money or shared access?

Contact your bank or payment provider immediately through its official app or a known number and ask what can be stopped or reversed. If you shared account credentials, use the service’s official recovery process. Report the incident through your local authorities’ official channels. Recovery is not guaranteed.
